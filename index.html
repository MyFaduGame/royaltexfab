Royal Tex Fab Industries

Founded in 2006, Royal TexFab Industries has built a strong legacy in textile manufacturing.

The company began with the production of polyester printed and dyed fabrics. Over the years, Royal TexFab has expanded significantly and now offers a diverse range of fabric varieties and products.

Since its inception, Royal TexFab has grown its manufacturing capabilities and developed an extensive product portfolio, including Single Jersey, Interlock, Modal, Loop Knit, and Cotton Spandex — with Cotton Spandex recognized as the company’s premium product line.
